About HostelCare

Hostel Room Complaint Tracker built for students and room maintenance tracking.

Project Overview

HostelCare is a responsive web-based Hostel Room Complaint Tracker that helps students submit room maintenance issues, search and filter complaints by category or status, track resolution progress, and view complete complaint records cleanly.

Dashboard Summary

Live counts for Total, Pending, In Progress, and Resolved complaints with quick status filtering[cite: 1].

Raise & Edit Form

Structured form with input validation, error alerts, and edit support for updating records[cite: 1].

Complaints Tracker

Search by room or ID, filter by category and status, and update progress directly[cite: 1].

Details & Remarks

Step-by-step progress timeline and technician or warden remarks for every complaint.
